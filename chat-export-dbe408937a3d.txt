1 | # OSS / Commercial Package Boundary
2 | 
3 | ## Rule
4 | - OSS packages MUST NOT depend on Commercial packages.
5 | - Verified by: `scripts/check-modular-boundaries.ps1`
6 | 
7 | ## Design Intent - Why Enterprise Source Is In This Public Repo
8 | 
9 | This repository uses a BUSL-style (Business Source License) open-core model:
10 | source code is publicly visible but usage of Commercial packages requires a
11 | valid commercial license. This is intentional, not an oversight.
12 | 
13 | Why source is public:
14 | - Transparency builds trust: customers can audit security and data-handling code before purchasing.
15 | - Forkability is a feature: OSS components (Core, RuleEngine, Tenancy) are freely forkable under Apache 2.0.
16 | - The real monetization moat is the SaaS Control Plane (private repos: `muonroi-control-plane`, `muonroi-license-server`), not the package source code.
17 | 
18 | What protects Commercial revenue:
19 | 1. RSA-signed ActivationProof - tier and capabilities are server-signed; you cannot forge them without the License Server private key.
20 | 2. Assembly hash verification - `CodeIntegrityVerifier` checks that all Muonroi.* assemblies in the process match the hashes approved at activation time. A fork that removes `EnsureFeatureOrThrow()` calls will fail integrity verification.
21 | 3. Heartbeat + grace period - revoked licenses degrade to Free tier after the configured grace period (24h Enterprise / 72h Licensed).
22 | 4. SaaS lock-in - hot-reload, approval workflows, canary rollout, and audit trail all require the private `muonroi-control-plane` service. These cannot be replicated by forking OSS packages.
23 | 
24 | What to do if you find a legitimate fork that bypasses commercial features:
25 | Report it at https://github.com/muonroi/muonroi-building-block/security/advisories/new.
26 | Per our Commercial License Agreement, production use without a valid license key is
27 | a license violation regardless of source visibility.
