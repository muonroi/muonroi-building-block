## CSS subset boundary (verified, RESEARCH)

Author ONLY declared v0.1 properties: block/inline, tables with `border-collapse:separate`, `@page`, `page-break-*`, `counter(page)`/`counter(pages)`, raster images (PNG/JPEG data-URI + IResourceResolver), `@font-face`, margins/orientation.
